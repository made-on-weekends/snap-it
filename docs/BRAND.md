# BRAND.md

> Identity layer: voice, naming, positioning. Actual values live in `DESIGN.md`.

## Naming & Logo
- **Product Name**: **SnapIt**
- **Brand Essence**: *The capture frame, and nothing else in the way.*
- **Logo**: **Capture Frame Mark** — two opposing corner brackets whose negative space defines the capture region. Single-fill monochrome, never dual-tone or clip art.
- **Wordmark**: Outlined from **Geist SemiBold** with optical kerning.

## Positioning
- **SnapIt** is a precise, client-side browser extension for web developers, QA testers, and SEO marketers who want to perform fast, bulk sequential website captures and annotations without third-party cloud dependencies or intrusive overlays.

## Voice
- The precise instrument: exact, quiet, predictable. Numbers are the copy. Verb-first actions.

### Voice rules
**Do:**
- State, don't sell ("Capture region", "1284 × 620", "Copied").
- Shortcuts over prose (`⌘ ⇧ 4` next to actions).
- Sentence case everywhere with explicit labels.
- Provide clear error reasons followed by actionable solutions.

**Don't:**
- Don't use exclamation marks in general product status logs or copy.
- Don't use marketing fluff ("Snap it in a flash!", "Perfect size!").
- No camera, scissors, or crop-tool clip art.
- No gradients, heavy glows, or glassmorphism on UI surfaces floating over page content.

## Brand-defining visual choices

Values live in [DESIGN.md](file:///mnt/workbench/repos/live/snap-it/docs/DESIGN.md).

- **Accent Color**: Cobalt (`#4E90F5` dark / `#1D57B8` light)
- **Neutral Palette**: Cool Graphite (`#0C0E12` dark bg to `#F6F7F9` light bg)
- **Heading / Wordmark Typeface**: `Geist` (SemiBold 600)
- **Body / UI Typeface**: `Inter` (Regular 400 / Medium 500)
- **Mono Typeface**: `Geist Mono` (Regular 400 / Medium 500)
